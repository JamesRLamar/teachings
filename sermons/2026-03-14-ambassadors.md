> "Now then, we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God were pleading through us: we implore you on Christ's behalf, be reconciled to God." (2 Corinthians 5:20)

### Super Ambassadors

Imagine Superman showed up at your door one day and asked you to be his ambassador. Superman wants to spend time with you every week talking about his plan to save the world, and he wants you to help him.

You are not Superman. You do not possess his power. But you know him. He has entrusted you with access to him. He has sent you in his name. You carry his authority, his message, and even the keys to his fortress. When you see a crisis, your job is not to draw attention to yourself, but to represent him, call on him, and act according to his will to save the world. That responsibility would carry weight. You could not live carelessly and still represent him well.

In a far greater way, the Christian life is this: we are not the King, but we belong to the King. We are not the source of power, but we are sent in His name and filled with His Spirit. We are kings and priests, entrusted with great responsibility and authority to carry out His will to save the world.

> "I, therefore, the prisoner of the Lord, beseech you to walk worthy of the calling with which you were called" (Ephesians 4:1)
>
> "that you may walk worthy of the Lord, fully pleasing Him, being fruitful in every good work and increasing in the knowledge of God" (Colossians 1:10)

**Are we walking worthily of the calling we have received?**

### 1. Ambassadors of the King

His eyes *were* like a flame of fire, and on His head *were* many crowns. He had a name written that no one knew except Himself. He *was* clothed with a robe dipped in blood, and His name is called The Word of God. And the armies in heaven, clothed in fine linen, white and clean, followed Him on white horses. Now out of His mouth goes a sharp sword, that with it He should strike the nations. And He Himself will rule them with a rod of iron. He Himself treads the winepress of the fierceness and wrath of Almighty God. And He has on *His* robe and on His thigh a name written:

> "Now then, we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God were pleading through us: we implore you on Christ's behalf, be reconciled to God." (2 Corinthians 5:20)

> "All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth. Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you; and lo, I am with you always, even to the end of the age. Amen." (Matthew 28:18-20)

Ambassadors don't live for **themselves**.

What does it look like to be an ambassador for Jesus in your school or work?

### 2. Being Kings

> "And have made us kings and priests to our God; And we shall reign on the earth." (Revelation 5:10)
>
> "But you are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, His own special people, that you may proclaim the praises of Him who called you out of darkness into His marvelous light" (1 Peter 2:9)
>
> "So He said to them, 'This kind can come out by nothing but prayer and fasting.'" (Mark 9:29)

> "Do you not know that we shall judge angels? How much more, things that pertain to this life?" (1 Corinthians 6:3)

We are called to be **kings and priests** and rule and reign with Jesus. We are called to walk in His Spirit and His **power.**

**How do we walk in His Spirit and His Power?**

### 3. Knowing the King

> "Now when they saw the boldness of Peter and John, and perceived that they were uneducated and untrained men, they marveled. And they realized that they had been with Jesus." (Acts 4:13)

> "And the evil spirit answered and said, 'Jesus I know, and Paul I know; but who are you?'" (Acts 19:15)

People can tell, even demons can tell if **we really know Jesus.**

**What are some ways for people to know that we walk with Jesus?**

### Reflection

- Are you living beneath the calling God has placed on your life?
- If we will one day judge angels, are we easily ruled by sin or by the opinions of others?
- Can people tell by your life that you have been with Jesus?
