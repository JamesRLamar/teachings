Every attitude has an audience, and God is always in the front row.

**Premise**: a bad attitude is sinful.

> "Now the men whom Moses sent to spy out the land, who returned and made all the congregation complain against him by bringing a bad report of the land..." (Numbers 14:36)

> "Say to them, 'As I live,' says the LORD, 'just as you have spoken in My hearing, so I will do to you...'" (Numbers 14:28)

That verdict is what cost Israel the Promised Land and caused an entire generation to suffer the consequence of their own attitude.

**What if all that is holding you back from God's promise is your attitude?**

## 1. Complaining is a verdict on God, not just on our circumstances

A grumbling and complaining spirit will poison you to the point where you'll even complain about God's miracles.

> "...for the LORD hears your complaints which you make against Him. And what are we? Your complaints are not against us but against the LORD." (Exodus 16:8)

> "nor complain, as some of them also complained, and were destroyed by the destroyer." (1 Corinthians 10:10)

**What does complaining say about our belief in God?**

**What circumstances excuse us from having a godly attitude?**

## 2. Every attitude has an audience.

> "Do all things without complaining and disputing, that you may become blameless and harmless, children of God without fault in the midst of a crooked and perverse generation, among whom you shine as lights in the world." (Philippians 2:14-15)

> "Let this mind be in you which was also in Christ Jesus..." (Philippians 2:5)

> "Not that I speak in regard to need, for I have **learned** in whatever state I am, to be content: I know how to be abased, and I know how to abound. Everywhere and in all things I have **learned** both to be full and to be hungry, both to abound and to suffer need. I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me." (Philippians 4:11-13)

Paul wrote this letter from prison after being beaten and ridiculed.

**How is contentment learned?**

> "Do not grumble against one another, brethren, lest you be condemned. Behold, the Judge is standing at the door!" (James 5:9)

**Thermometer vs. Thermostat**: A thermometer only reports the temperature of the room. A thermostat sets it. A carnal mind is a thermometer; it reflects whatever mood, news, or person is in the room. A spiritual mind is a thermostat; it sets the temperature by deciding in advance where it will focus.

## 3. Every action has an audience.

> "And whatever you do, do it heartily, as to the Lord and not to men, knowing that from the Lord you will receive the reward of the inheritance; for you serve the Lord Christ." (Colossians 3:23-24)

> "Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly... singing with grace in your hearts to the Lord. And whatever you do in word or deed, **do all in the name of the Lord Jesus**, giving thanks to God the Father through Him." (Colossians 3:16-17)

## 4. The mind is like a compass

A mind is like a compass; you have to check and correct it throughout the day.

> "You will keep him in perfect peace, whose **mind is stayed on You**, because he trusts in You." (Isaiah 26:3)

> "Rejoice always, pray without ceasing, **in everything give thanks**; for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus for you." (1 Thessalonians 5:16-18)

> "This is the day the LORD has made; **we will rejoice and be glad in it**." (Psalm 118:24)

> "Keep your heart **with all diligence**, for out of it spring the issues of life." (Proverbs 4:23)

> "For to be carnally minded is death, but to be spiritually minded is life and peace." (Romans 8:6)

**If life and peace are the fruit of a spiritual mind, what does the absence of peace in our week reveal about where our mind has been set?**

## Meditation Questions

- **How often can you honestly say your day is marked by life and peace?**
- **What will it take for you to start setting your mind on spiritual things? What has to happen every day or multiple times a day for that to be true every day?**
- **What actions are being done for the wrong audience with the wrong attitude?**
- **If someone recorded every complaint or bad attitude you had this week, what would they conclude about your view of God?**
- **Is your attitude acting more like a thermometer or a thermostat right now?**
