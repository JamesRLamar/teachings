Faithfulness is believing that saying no to this pursuit is not an option. Do not forsake the assembling, remember the sabbath day. The sabbath is a test of both faithfulness and self-control. It's about saying yes to time with Him and no to selfish desire.

Self-control is easy to say for tomorrow, very hard to execute today. proximity corelates to difficulty. We can see the need for self-control in the past or in the future but once it's upon us all of a sudden the need for self-control gets muddy. This is is when our capacity for creative excuses becomes supernatural. Finances belong in this category.



Self-control begets self-control and the reverse is true as well. We need to spend time developing a growth mindset that we can do hard things. This is why "putting your shoes" on as a practice works, because it builds confidence that we can do things we don't want to do. And over time we can do increasingly hard things we don't want to do.

There are things we think we want, but we know we don't like.



Being full of good stuff is also a clever hack to reduce the noise of temptation.

Self-control is investing in the future self. If you don't like yourself or your situation today, just look at yesterday.
