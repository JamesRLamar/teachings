### Which Mark Do You Bear?

Revelation 14:9–11:

> "If anyone worships the beast and his image, and receives his mark on his forehead or on his hand, he himself shall also drink of the wine of the wrath of God, which is poured out full strength into the cup of His indignation. He shall be tormented with fire and brimstone in the presence of the holy angels and in the presence of the Lamb. And the smoke of their torment ascends forever and ever; and they have no rest day or night, who worship the beast and his image, and whoever receives the mark of his name."

Two things are coming that the church at large is not ready for: persecution, and the judgment that follows it.

### The Man with the Marker and an Axe

*Read Ezekiel 9:1-7*

> "Then He called out in my hearing with a loud voice, saying, 'Let those who have charge over the city draw near, each with a deadly weapon in his hand.' And suddenly six men came from the direction of the upper gate, which faces north, each with his battle-ax in his hand. One man among them was clothed with linen and had a writer's inkhorn at his side. They went in and stood beside the bronze altar. Now the glory of the God of Israel had gone up from the cherub, where it had been, to the threshold of the temple. And He called to the man clothed with linen, who had the writer's inkhorn at his side; and the LORD said to him, 'Go through the midst of the city, through the midst of Jerusalem, and put a mark on the foreheads of the men who sigh and cry over all the abominations that are done within it.' To the others He said in my hearing, 'Go after him through the city and kill; do not let your eye spare, nor have any pity. Utterly slay old and young men, maidens and little children and women; but do not come near anyone on whom is the mark; and begin at My sanctuary.' So they began with the elders who were before the temple." (Ezekiel 9:1-7)

The man with the inkhorn did not ask anyone what they believed. He looked for what they felt about sin.

Now hear the same scene at the end of the Time:

> "Then I saw another angel ascending from the east, having the seal of the living God. And he cried with a loud voice to the four angels to whom it was granted to harm the earth and the sea, saying, 'Do not harm the earth, the sea, or the trees till we have sealed the servants of our God on their foreheads.'" (Revelation 7:2-3)

> "He causes all, both small and great, rich and poor, free and slave, to receive a mark on their right hand or on their foreheads, and that no one may buy or sell except one who has the mark or the name of the beast, or the number of his name." (Revelation 13:16-17)

Two marks. There is no unmarked crowd in the middle.

**If the man with the marker walked through this room today, how would you be marked?**

### 1. Nobody is unmarked

> "And these words which I command you today shall be in your heart. You shall teach them diligently to your children, and shall talk of them when you sit in your house, when you walk by the way, when you lie down, and when you rise up. You shall bind them as a sign on your hand, and they shall be as frontlets between your eyes." (Deuteronomy 6:6-8)

Long before the apostle John saw the vision of the Mark of the Beast, God had already described how His people should be marked. On the hand and the forehead, or heart as a later verse says. The hand represents what we do. The forehead/heart represents what we think about most, therefore what we love most. Everyone of us is marked by whatever governs our hands and our hearts.

That means the question is not "Will I choose a Mark someday?" It is "Which Mark am I choosing right now?"

**We do not decide our Mark on the day of judgment; we reveal the one we have been choosing all along.**

**What does the world say about making Jesus exclusive?**

### 2. The Beast's Mark is a test of provision

> "...that no one may buy or sell except one who has the mark or the name of the beast, or the number of his name." (Revelation 13:17)

> "No one can serve two masters; for either he will hate the one and love the other, or else he will be loyal to the one and despise the other. You cannot serve God and mammon." (Matthew 6:24)

Look at what the final test is tied to, it's noot a gun to the head, it's a test of trusting God's Provison—Buying and selling. Groceries. Rent. Your job. Your ability to provide for your family.

It is such a simple test. And it is being given to us right now, every week, in smaller doses. When keeping God's commandments costs money, which way do we lean? When honesty costs a sale, when integrity costs a promotion.

If we will not lose a little money for God today, we will not lose our livelihood for Him then.

**The one who cannot say no to Mammon now has already said yes to the Beast.**

**When was the last time obeying God cost you something?**

### 3. The Sabbath is God's weekly rehearsal for the test

> "Then the LORD said to Moses, 'Behold, I will rain bread from heaven for you. And the people shall go out and gather a certain quota every day, that I may test them, whether they will walk in My law or not.'" (Exodus 16:4)

> "Speak also to the children of Israel, saying: "Surely My Sabbaths you shall keep, for it is a sign between Me and you throughout your generations, that you may know that I am the LORD who sanctifies you."'" (Exodus 31:13)

God did not test Israel's faith with a theology exam. He tested it with bread and a day off. "Gather six days, and on the seventh, stop, and trust Me to have already provided." Some went out on the seventh day anyway, because they did not believe the sixth day's bread was enough, they did not trust in God's provision. And God commanded them to be stoned.

God calls the Sabbath a sign between Him and His people. A sign is a mark. Every week He hands us the same test the beast will one day hand the world: Will you stop buying and selling and trust Me to provide? Guarding the Sabbath is not legalism. It is practicing for the day when trusting God with our provision will cost everything.

And it is not only the Sabbath. Do we protect our daily time with Him, or does it evaporate the moment the schedule gets busy? 

Daniel prayed at his window when it was illegal because he had prayed at his window every day when it was not.

**What are some small ways we can put God first every week to prepare us for when it will cost everything?**

### 4. The Marked assemble together

> "And let us consider one another in order to stir up love and good works, not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together, as is the manner of some, but exhorting one another, and so much the more as you see the Day approaching." (Hebrews 10:24-25)

> "Here is the patience of the saints; here are those who keep the commandments of God and the faith of Jesus." (Revelation 14:12)

Notice that Hebrews ties assembling/gathering together directly to the Day of Judgment approaching. The closer judgment comes, the more we need each other, not less. Imagine the church building doors are shut. Imagine biblical teaching, worship music, or Christian content on the internet become illegal overnight. What would remain?

If what remains is a group of people who know each other's names, break bread in their homes, and pray for each other by name, then we have a church that will stand when persecution comes.

Gathering as a house church is a real buffer against institutional persecution. But it is not a shield against the daily test. However, the marked are found together, and they are found keeping the commandments of God and holding the faith of Jesus.

**Investing our time in God's people now is how we become a church that survives when everything else is taken away.**

### Closing

The man with the Marker and the Axe did not mark the people who attended the temple. He marked the people who sighed and cried over breaking God's Commandments. The Mark of God in Revelation is on those who keep His commandments and the faith of Jesus. The Mark of the beast is on everyone who decided their groceries mattered more than their God.

- Is the Sabbath, and your daily time with God, protected, or is it the first thing traded when inconvenient?
- How well have you invested yourself in this community? How well do you know and are known by others?
- Do you take God's side now, in the small things?
- Do you sigh and cry over sin to the point that you actually walk away from it rather than back to it?
- If God walked through this room today, which mark would he find you already wearing?
