# Whose Life Is It? - Brainstorm

## Main Idea

**Golden Apple:** Your life is a finite resource, and where you spend your time, money, and strength reveals where your treasure really is and whom you truly trust.

**Layers the message can move through:**

1. **Treasure vs. Testimony** - What we claim with our mouth is tested by what receives our best time, energy, and money.
2. **Anxiety vs. Trust** - Worry exposes the places where we still believe our future depends on our management instead of God's care.
3. **Ownership vs. Stewardship** - Because we are not our own, our body, plans, work, and resources must be brought under Christ's rule.

## Main Points

### 1. Your treasure directs your heart, and your life follows your treasure

> "Do not lay up for yourselves treasures on earth, where moth and rust destroy and where thieves break in and steal; but lay up for yourselves treasures in heaven... For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also." (Matthew 6:19-21, NKJV)

**Your calendar, spending, and attention preach a more honest sermon about your heart than your words do.**

**If someone studied your last month of time and money, what would they conclude you treasure most?**

### 2. Ordinary work must stay under Christ, not replace Christ

> "And whatever you do, do it heartily, as to the Lord and not to men, knowing that from the Lord you will receive the reward of the inheritance; for you serve the Lord Christ." (Colossians 3:23-24, NKJV)

**Work, school, and responsibility can be acts of worship, but they become idolatry when they consume what belongs to God, family, and mission.**

**Where are you giving your best strength so fully to work or school that Jesus, your family, and disciple-making are getting the leftovers?**

### 3. Worry reveals where trust is still misplaced

> "Therefore I say to you, do not worry about your life, what you will eat or what you will drink; nor about your body, what you will put on... But seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness, and all these things shall be added to you." (Matthew 6:25, 33, NKJV)

**Anxiety often shows that we are trying to secure by control what God has asked us to entrust by faith.**

**What recurring worry is exposing an area you have not actually entrusted to God?**

### 4. Because you are not your own, your life must be budgeted for God's purposes

> "You are not your own... therefore glorify God in your body and in your spirit, which are God's." (1 Corinthians 6:19-20, NKJV)

**Discipleship begins when you stop treating your life as private property and start treating it as holy stewardship.**

**What are you still spending as though it were yours alone: your body, your future, your money, or your reputation?**

### 5. Following Jesus means saying no to rival claims on your life

> "If anyone desires to come after Me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross daily, and follow Me." (Luke 9:23, NKJV)

**You cannot give yourself fully to ambition, approval, comfort, or endless social claims and still give yourself fully to Jesus.**

**What would "take up your cross" require you to subtract from your life this week so you can more fully follow Christ?**

## Illustrations

- **Tax Season Audit** (opening illustration): Around tax season, people gather statements, receipts, and records because numbers tell the truth. You can ignore them all year, but when you audit the books, patterns appear. In the same way, a spiritual audit of our calendar, bank account, and thought life reveals where our treasure really is.

## Other Scriptures

- **Proverbs 3:5-6** - Trust in the Lord with all your heart and acknowledge Him in all your ways; this presses against compartmentalized decision-making.
- **James 4:13-17** - Planning life as if God were optional exposes practical atheism, and verse 17 sharpens the warning by showing that omission is itself sin.
- **Romans 12:1-2** - Present your body as a living sacrifice; the whole life belongs on the altar, not just church activity.
- **Psalm 90:12** - Number our days; a finite life should make us deliberate with time and priorities.
- **Ephesians 5:15-17** - Redeem the time; wisdom is seen in how carefully a believer spends his days.
- **Haggai 1:5-9** - Misordered priorities produce spiritual leanness even when activity increases.
- **Psalm 24:1** - "The earth is the Lord's"; stewardship starts with the recognition that nothing is truly ours.

## Possible Closing Questions

1. **If my money, time, and attention reveal my treasure, what are they saying about my heart?**
2. **What part of my stress is coming from carrying what only God was meant to carry?**
3. **What recurring worry is exposing an area you have not actually entrusted to God?**
4. **What in my life is consistently getting my best while God, family, and mission receive what is left?**
5. **What one concrete change in my calendar, spending, or commitments would show that Christ truly has first place?**
