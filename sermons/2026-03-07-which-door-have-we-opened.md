# Which Door Have We Opened?

---

## Intro

Two doors. One crouches at the threshold, waiting. The other stands outside, knocking. Both want in. Only one brings life.

> "If you do well, will you not be accepted? And if you do not do well, sin lies at the door. And its desire is for you, but you should rule over it." (Genesis 4:7, NKJV)

> "Behold, I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears My voice and opens the door, I will come in to him and dine with him, and he with Me." (Revelation 3:20, NKJV)

> "I am the door. If anyone enters by Me, he will be saved, and will go in and out and find pasture." (John 10:9, NKJV)

**Which door have we opened?**

---

## Main Idea

**Golden Apple:** Every choice is a door — we either open to sin, which crouches and desires us, or to Jesus, who knocks and offers Himself as the door to life.

**The Two Doors:**
1. **The door for sin** — Genesis 4:7. Sin lies at the door. Its desire is for us. We are meant to rule over it — but we must not open it.
2. **The door for Jesus** — Revelation 3:20; John 10:9. He stands and knocks. He *is* the door. We must open to Him.

---

## Main Points

### 1. Sin Lies at the Door — and Desires Us

> "If you do well, will you not be accepted? And if you do not do well, sin lies at the door. And its desire is for you, but you should rule over it." (Genesis 4:7, NKJV)

**Sin doesn't knock. It crouches. It waits. Its desire is for us — to master us, to consume us.**

**What door have we left cracked — or swung wide — that sin has already slipped through?**

---

### 2. Jesus Stands at the Door and Knocks

> "Behold, I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears My voice and opens the door, I will come in to him and dine with him, and he with Me." (Revelation 3:20, NKJV)

**Jesus does not force His way in. He stands. He knocks. He waits for us to open.**

**When was the last time we heard His voice at the door — and chose to open?**

---

### 3. Jesus Is the Door — the Only Way In

> "I am the door. If anyone enters by Me, he will be saved, and will go in and out and find pasture." (John 10:9, NKJV)

**Jesus is not only the one knocking — He is the door itself. Salvation, safety, and pasture are found only through Him.**

**Are we trying to enter life through any other door — achievement, religion, self-improvement — instead of through Him?**

---

### 4. We Cannot Open Both

We cannot serve two masters. We cannot open the door to sin and expect to rule over it. We cannot ignore the knock and expect the feast.

**Which door have we opened — and which door have we left closed?**

## Closing

Two doors. One crouches. One knocks. One desires to devour. One desires to dine. The choice is ours — and we make it every day.

- When did you last hear Jesus knocking — and did you open?
